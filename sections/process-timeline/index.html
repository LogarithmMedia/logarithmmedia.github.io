Process and timeline.

A reusable overview of the steps in a digital project. The sequence is illustrative and the scope is agreed during consultation.

HOW WE WORK

From first conversation to continuous improvement.

Understand the business, choose the right channels, build carefully and review results together.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Understand your business, audience, goals and existing website or campaigns.

    Focus Growth audit

  2. 02

    Plan

    Agree on priorities, channels, scope and the measures that matter to your business.

    Focus Project plan

  3. 03

    Build and launch

    Prepare the agreed website, content or campaigns and check the experience before launch.

    Focus Launch readiness

  4. 04

    Review and improve

    Review performance, share reporting and use the findings to refine the next steps.

    Focus Ongoing review

Timing depends on the agreed project scope.
