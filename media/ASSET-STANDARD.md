# Media asset standard

Owner direction: reusable creative illustrations must work in both light and dark themes. Export as WebP with genuine alpha (not a solid or baked-in gradient background). Inspect decoded alpha, including transparent corner pixels and a meaningful transparent region; an alpha channel alone is insufficient. Review actual composites on #F6F8FC and #0A1320 for halos, clipping, edge visibility and legibility. Retain natural local shadows when appropriate. Full-bleed backgrounds and documentary photography are separate asset categories and may be opaque; they must be clearly labelled. Existing opaque drafts are not approved for reuse as theme-independent illustrations. Assign stable LM-MEDIA IDs and new variant IDs for replacements after publication. Record actual dimensions, byte count, SHA-256, source type, alpha status, alt text and approval status. Verify ZIP integrity and individual checksums before importing. Keep gallery and catalog synchronized, publish draft review only, and verify public image loading. Owner approval is required before production use. Contact-sheet crops are draft card-size candidates, not high-resolution standalone artwork.
